# Prior Art and Acknowledgements

This repository builds on a body of prior work that established
the quantum nature of QCA.  The specific results in this
repository are the authors' own contribution; the underlying
foundation is not.

## Foundations

- **Wu, L.-A. and Zhang, D.-L.** (2005). *Coherent quantum-dot
  cellular automata.* Europhys. Lett. 71, 524. — Introduced
  coherent QCA, entanglement generation, and CNOT gates from
  the native coupling.

- **Xu, H.-Q., Feng, G.-R. and Zhang, D.-L.** (2008).
  *Universal quantum computation with QCA.* arXiv:0801.4617. —
  Framed QCA as a universal quantum computation platform.

- **Brennen, G. K. and Williams, J. E.** (2003). *Entanglement
  dynamics in QCA.* arXiv:quant-ph/0306056. — Established that
  entanglement spreads through a QCA array.

- **Feng, G.-R., Xu, H.-Q. and Zhang, D.-L.** (2007). *Entangled
  photon generation from QCA.* J. Phys.: Condens. Matter 19,
  326215.

- **Anonymous** (2026). *VQE of molecular QCA on IBM hardware.*
  J. Appl. Phys. 139, 024401. — Closest hardware prior art.

## What this repository adds

The following are the authors' original contributions:

- STA (counter-diabatic) clocking of QCA with 5000× speedup
- Toric code and Kitaev honeycomb on a QCA lattice
- Fermi-Hubbard simulation on a QCA lattice
- Measurement-induced phase transitions in QCA chains
- Toric-code stabilizer measurement on IBM Marrakesh
- The full software stack implementing all of the above
